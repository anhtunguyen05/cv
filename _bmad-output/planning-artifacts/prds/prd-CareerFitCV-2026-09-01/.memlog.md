- (decision) MVP boundary: structured CV, deterministic JD matching, template preview, and browser print/HTML export; AI interview, AI patches, provider integrations, and advanced orchestration are later phases.
- (decision) CV editing uses a mutable draft profile plus immutable named versions.
- (decision) Deterministic matching is the official MVP behavior and does not require an LLM.
- (decision) Browser print/HTML export is the initial export path; server-side or worker PDF export is later.
- (event) PRD finalized after source reconciliation, decision confirmation, FR/UJ/SM consistency review, and rubric review.
